# Title : E-commerce-customer-analysis

## Project Overview

1. This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce customer behavior, spending patterns and satisfaction levels using Excel, Python, MySQL, and Power BI.

2. The objective is to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights that can help businesses understand customer segments, evaluate purchasing behavior, and identify customers who may require retention-focused attention.

3. The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to SQL-based business analysis and interactive dashboard development.


## Business Problem

E-commerce businesses need to understand how customers spend, how satisfied they are, and which customer segments may require additional attention.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- Which membership type contributes the highest total spending?
- How is customer satisfaction distributed across the customer base?
- How does customer distribution vary across cities?
- Which age group contributes the highest total spending?
- Which customers may be at risk based on purchase recency and satisfaction?
- Which high-spending customers may require retention-focused attention?
- What business actions can be considered based on the findings?
   

## Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare customer data for analysis.
- Perform exploratory data analysis to identify patterns and trends.
- Analyze customer behavior using SQL queries.
- Compare spending and satisfaction across customer segments.
- Identify potential at-risk customers using defined criteria.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Present actionable, data-driven business recommendations.

## Key Findings

Based on the current dashboard observations:

1.Membership: Gold members contributed the highest total spending, reaching 12.8M.
2.Satisfaction: 39.1% of customers were satisfied, while 23.9% reported dissatisfaction.
3.City Analysis: Customer distribution remained nearly equal across all six cities.
4.Age Group: Customers aged 56+ generated the highest total spending of 9.7M.
5.Customer Base: The analysis covered approximately 100K customer records to understand purchasing behavior.


## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can be considered:

1. **Membership Strategy:** Investigate the factors contributing to Gold members' spending and evaluate whether similar strategies could benefit other membership segments.
2. **Customer Satisfaction:** Examine feedback from neutral and unsatisfied customers to identify possible service or experience improvements.
3. **Customer Retention:** Investigate potential at-risk customers and consider targeted engagement or retention campaigns.
4. **High-Value Customer Focus:** Prioritize further analysis of high-spending customers who also meet the risk criteria.
5. **Customer Segmentation:** Use city, age group, and membership attributes to understand differences in customer behavior.
6. **Performance Monitoring:** Track customer satisfaction, spending, and retention-related indicators over time to evaluate business actions.
